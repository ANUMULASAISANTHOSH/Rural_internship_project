# AQUILA: Urban Flood Intelligence, Infrastructure Monitoring & Emergency Response Platform

AQUILA is an academic geospatial decision-support prototype designed to support pluvial flood risk assessment, infrastructure monitoring, incident management, and flood-aware emergency dispatch routing. The project uses the Hyderabad Metropolitan Region, Telangana, India as its case-study domain, with **Manikonda** as its initial implementation and demonstration catchment.

### Non-Authoritative Prototype Notice

AQUILA is an academic decision-support prototype developed for research and educational purposes. It is not intended to replace statutory disaster management bodies such as the Hyderabad Disaster Response and Asset Protection Agency (HYDRAA), the Greater Hyderabad Municipal Corporation (GHMC), the Telangana State Disaster Management Authority (TGSDMA), or the India Meteorological Department (IMD). AQUILA does not issue official public flood forecasts or legally binding emergency directives.
